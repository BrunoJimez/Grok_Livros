Judea Pearl is professor of computer science at the University of California, Los Angeles, winner of the 2011 Turing Award, and author of three classic technical books on causality. He lives in Los Angeles, California. Dana Mackenzie is an award-winning science writer and author of The Big Splat, or How Our Moon Came to Be. He lives in Santa Cruz, California.
